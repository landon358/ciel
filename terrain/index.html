What is a custom topographic map print?

A map of a place you choose, drawn from its real elevation. Ciel prints it as shaded relief, where slopes catch light and cast shadow, or as fine contour lines. You pick the area, the colors and the title.

Can I make a map of my ski town or home mountain?

Yes. Search any US town, ski area, peak or national park and the studio frames it for you. The map shows the shape of the mountain, its ridges and bowls, rather than ski runs or lifts.

Does it make a good gift?

It is one of the most personal gifts you can give: the lake where they grew up, the trail where you got engaged, the mountain they ski every winter. You see a watermarked preview before it prints, or choose the digital file and print it yourself.

What sizes and prices are there?

Prints are 12 x 16, 18 x 24 or 24 x 32 inches, from $49 unframed or $135 framed in black wood. The digital file is $15. A second map in the same order is 25% off.
