Questions.

Does the map show ski runs or lifts?

No. Ciel maps the land itself: the ridges, bowls and valleys of Big Sky, traced from lidar. Runs and lifts are not drawn, so the print reads as a portrait of your home mountain rather than a trail map. Roads and the town can be shown or hidden.

What is a shaded relief map?

A map where the land is lit from one side so slopes catch light and cast shadow. Instead of reading contour numbers, you see the shape of the terrain the way you would from a plane.

Where does the elevation data come from?

The U.S. Geological Survey 3D Elevation Program. Where the USGS has flown lidar, the map is built from one meter measurements; elsewhere it uses their 10 meter elevation model. This Big Sky print uses 1 m lidar.

Can I change the colors, area or title?

Yes. The studio opens on this exact Big Sky map. You can pick any of 12 palettes or a custom one, switch between shaded relief and contour lines, zoom from 1.5 to 25 km across, move the frame, mark a spot and edit both title lines.

Do I see it before it prints?

Yes. Every print order gets a watermarked preview by email first, and you have 24 hours to ask for changes before it goes to the printer. The digital file is a 7200 by 9600 pixel image, sharp enough for 24 by 32 inches.
